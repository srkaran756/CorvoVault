# Product Philosophy

CorvoVault is a local study workspace for people who collect, read, and work with learning materials such as PDFs, links, videos, and notes.

## Product problem

Learning materials are often scattered across file systems, browsers, PDF readers, and note-taking tools. CorvoVault aims to reduce the cost of finding, reading, annotating, and revisiting those materials in one local desktop application.

## Current product direction

The current product is primarily a local material-management and reading application. Its longer-term direction is to help users connect materials and build understanding, but that direction should not be confused with the current feature set.

## Principles

- Local storage is the default for vault data.
- Network access should be visible and attributable to a feature or configured provider.
- Implemented behavior should be documented separately from planned behavior.
- Features should reduce friction in reading, organizing, or revisiting materials.
- Technical complexity should be justified by a user or maintenance benefit.
- A feature is not complete until its normal, empty, slow, and failure states are understood.

## Scope boundaries

CorvoVault is not currently a complete learning platform, cloud synchronization service, mobile companion, or general-purpose AI assistant. Those may be future directions or research areas, but they are not current product guarantees.
